Where to find your files

  • Garmin Connect: open an activity → ⚙ → Export Original (a .zip containing the .fit — unzip it first) or Export to TCX/GPX.
  • COROS: in the app, open an activity → share → Export data → .fit.
  • Strava: an activity's ⋯ menu → Export GPX or Export Original.
  • Timing systems / lap counters: a .csv with a date column and split times (and optionally distance and heart rate) per lap.
